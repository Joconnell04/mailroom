Privacy policy

Mailroom is a personal email client that runs on its owner's own computer. This page explains what it does with data from Google accounts connected to it.

What Mailroom accesses

  • Gmail (read, label, archive and move to trash), to show the signed-in user's mail and let them organize it.
  • Google Sheets, to add sorting rules to a spreadsheet the user owns and to show why an email was sorted the way it was.

Where the data goes

  • Everything runs on the owner's computer. Mailroom has no server of its own, and email content is never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in a file on that computer readable only by its user. Email content is fetched from Google when it is displayed and kept only in a local cache.
  • Mailroom has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and never sells or shares data.
  • Opening an email can load pictures from the sender's own servers, as in any mail app.

Google API data

Mailroom's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove an account in Mailroom's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Mailroom's folder removes everything it stored.
